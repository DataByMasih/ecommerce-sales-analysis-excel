# E-Commerce Sales Analysis — Excel

This is my first data analysis project, built using Microsoft Excel.

I started with a small e-commerce sales dataset and tried to answer some basic business questions around sales, profit, regions, products, categories, and discounts.

## Business Questions

The main questions I wanted to explore were:

- How are sales changing month by month?
- Which region is performing best?
- Which categories and products generate the most sales?
- Which regions have better profit margins?
- How do different discount levels relate to profit?

## Dataset

The dataset contains 500 orders and 10 columns:

- Order ID
- Date
- Region
- Category
- Product
- Customer
- Sales
- Quantity
- Profit
- Discount

## Data Cleaning

Before starting the analysis, I checked the data for basic quality issues.

Some of the steps I performed:

- Checked for blank values
- Checked for duplicate Order IDs
- Removed a duplicate order
- Cleaned the date format
- Checked numeric fields
- Converted the Discount column to percentage format

## Analysis

I used Excel PivotTables and PivotCharts to analyze:

- Monthly sales
- Sales by region
- Sales by category
- Top-selling products
- Profit by region
- Profit margin by region
- Profit margin by category
- Discount levels and their relationship with profit

Profit margin was calculated as:

`Profit Margin = Profit / Sales`

## Key Findings

- North had the highest sales, profit, and profit margin among the regions.
- Electronics generated the highest total sales and profit.
- Office Supplies had the highest profit margin among the categories.
- The 20% discount group had the lowest total profit among the discount levels compared.
- Headphones, Laptop, and Keyboard were the top three products by sales.

## Dashboard

The final dashboard brings the main results together and includes:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Profit Margin
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Region-wise Sales
- Category-wise Sales
- Top 3 Products
- Key Insights
- Recommendations

## Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

- Look at what is working well in the North region and see whether similar approaches can be applied to weaker regions.
- Continue focusing on Electronics while looking for ways to improve its profit margin.
- Explore ways to increase sales of Office Supplies because of its relatively strong margin.
- Review heavy discounting, especially the 20% discount level.
